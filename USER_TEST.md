# PharmaSignal: five-minute user test

The deployment brief asks that someone outside the team tests the app. Do this once before the presentation.

## Who to ask
A pharmacist, procurement officer or classmate who has **not** seen PharmaSignal. One person is enough; two is better.

## How to run it
1. Open the live app on **their** phone or laptop. Do not drive it for them.
2. Read the three tasks below aloud, one at a time.
3. **Do not help or explain.** Watch where they hesitate, misread or get stuck, and write it down.
4. Stop after five minutes, even if they have not finished.

## The three tasks
1. "You are about to award a metformin tender. Check what is known about these bidders: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Lupin Pharmaceuticals and Marksans Pharma."
2. "Has anything unusual happened with recalls in the last few months?"
3. "Ask the app whether Teva Pharmaceuticals USA has had serious recalls."

## Questions to ask afterwards
- In one sentence, what is this app for?
- Which part was confusing or slow?
- Would you trust what it told you? Why or why not?
- What would you do next with the tender result?
